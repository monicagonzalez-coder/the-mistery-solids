Exit challenge: explain the models

  1. Why does solid NaCl not conduct electricity, whereas molten or aqueous NaCl does?
  2. Predict the formula for calcium nitride using the charges of Ca²⁺ and N³⁻.
  3. Why is the melting point of MgO much higher than that of NaCl?
  4. Evaluate: “All ionic compounds conduct electricity because they contain charged particles.”
  5. Is the formula NaCl a formula for an individual molecule? Explain.
